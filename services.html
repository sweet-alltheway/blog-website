APP DESIGN

Intuitive Designs for Smarter Farming

Landing Page
The landing page is the first screen users see when they open the app. Its main goal is to quickly introduce what AgroTasker is about and guide users to start using it.
Login Page
The Login Page is where users enter their credentials (like email and password) to access their account. It should feel simple, welcoming, and easy to use especially since users like farmers or task managers need quick access.
Home Page
The Home Page is the main dashboard where users land after logging in. It should give a quick overview of their tasks and activities, and make it easy to navigate to other parts of the app.
Task Page
The Task Page is where users can see all their tasks, manage them, and track their progress.
Weather Page
The Weather Page gives users real-time (or at least daily updated) weather information, which is super important for farming tasks.
Profile Page

Crop Scan Page
Users scan and monitor their crops for various purposes, like growth tracking, pest detection, or disease identification.
Settings Page
The Settings Page is where users can customize their app experience and manage preferences. It should be easy to navigate and have all the essential options for personalizing the app.
Chatbot Page
The Chatbot Page could be a really useful feature in AgroTasker, providing users with an interactive way to get assistance, get task recommendations, or even answer farming-related questions quickly
